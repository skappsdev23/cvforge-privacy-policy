Privacy Policy

CVForge - Resume Maker

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CVForge - Resume Maker handles information when you use the application.

By using CVForge - Resume Maker, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information You Enter

CVForge allows you to enter information such as your name, job title, email address, phone number, address, LinkedIn profile, website, education, work experience, skills, projects, and other resume information.

This information is used to create, display, save, edit, and generate your resume within the application.

2. Profile Photos

If you choose to add a profile photo, the application uses the photo selected from your device for your resume.

The photo may be cropped or processed locally on your device. You control whether a profile photo is added to your resume.

3. Resume Storage

Saved resumes are stored locally on your device using the application's local storage features.

CVForge does not require you to create an account or log in to use the resume-making features.

4. PDF Generation

When you generate a PDF resume, the document is created from the information you entered into the application.

You may use Android's available sharing, saving, printing, and file-management features to manage the generated PDF.

5. Advertising

CVForge uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and use information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, and information related to advertising interactions, subject to Google's applicable policies and settings.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on applicable settings, consent requirements, and Google's advertising systems.

6. Third-Party Services

CVForge uses third-party services, including Google AdMob for advertising.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

CVForge does not sell your resume information to third parties.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

CVForge is not specifically directed toward children. The application should not be used by children below the minimum age required by applicable laws or platform rules.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be made available through the published Privacy Policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or CVForge - Resume Maker, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
